Say hello to Crystaline.

## What is Crystaline? Crystaline is a work in progress Vulkan based rendering engine written in C++, mainly serving as a replacement to Electron but could later be the rendering engine for QuickSand or Revie.
There isn't much to show yet, but you can check out the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/the-lstv/crystaline) for more information.
